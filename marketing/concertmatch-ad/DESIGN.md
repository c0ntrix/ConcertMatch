# ConcertMatch — Unser Abend

## Style Prompt
A concert poster brought to life: quiet editorial confidence, enormous type, physical record grooves, two different visual rhythms resolving into one shared night. Warm paper and almost-black, with olive as the shared sound. Precise Swiss composition meets a personal, italic voice. High energy comes from choreography and scale, never decoration.

## Colors
- Paper #fdfdfb: brand ground and typography on dark scenes.
- Charcoal #272824: brand ink and evening ground.
- Olive #b7c79d: shared sound, intersection, reveal.
- Clay #cfb69c: the second person's warmer musical identity.
- Muted #707269: small explanatory text on paper only.

## Typography
Instrument Sans variable: bold statements, pragmatic product interface.
Instrument Serif italic: the other person's voice and the emotional payoff.
The tension embodies two people with different tastes. Headlines 150–240 px; support 32–46 px; captions 26 px minimum. Tightly tracked sans, open serif.

## Motion
30 seconds, 1920×1080, 60 fps, 120 BPM original score. First animation at 0.15 s. Directional reveals are primary; a circular aperture opens the crowd at the climax. Distinct tempos: snappy glyphs, medium card movement, slow photographic drift. Every scene has an animated entrance and a visible transition. All motion seekable on one paused GSAP timeline.

## Composition
96 px landscape safe margin. Full-size scene-content containers with padding. Decoratives may intentionally bleed; meaningful text must remain within safe areas. Match graphic is a metaphor, never a numerical compatibility promise. No fictional concert dates or prices.

## What NOT to Do
No neon purple, gratuitous gradients, bouncing UI, stock laptop mockups, fabricated match scores, paid feature claims, generic slideshow fades or voiceover. No Spotify login claim. No artificial artist endorsements. Preserve the understated ConcertMatch wordmark.
